Built for You.

Oye Ride was born out of a simple frustration — getting around Kasoa shouldn't be stressful. Whether you're a student trying to beat traffic to campus, a business owner shipping products, or someone sending a gift across town, transportation should work for you, not against you.

We built Oye Ride from scratch in Ghana, for Ghanaians. Our riders are local professionals who know the streets. Our technology — from live GPS tracking to surge-aware pricing — is designed to make every trip fair, fast, and reliable.
