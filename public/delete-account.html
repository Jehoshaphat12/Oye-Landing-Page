Privacy & Data Control

Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. You can request full deletion of your Oye Ride account and all associated personal data. We'll process your request promptly and confirm once it's complete.

This page complies with Google Play's account deletion requirements and GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure).

Delete from the App

If you still have the Oye Ride app installed, the fastest way is to delete your account directly from within the app.

  1. 1 Open the Oye Ride app and sign in
  2. 2 Go to Profile → Settings → Account
  3. 3 Tap "Delete Account" and confirm

Your data will be removed from Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and all associated services within 7 days.

Request via Email

Don't have the app anymore? Send us a deletion request from the email address linked to your Oye Ride account.

Data Scope

What We Delete

Here's exactly what gets removed when you delete your account.

Account Profile

Name, email, phone number, password, and all profile fields — deleted from Firebase Auth.

Trip History

All ride and delivery records, pickup/drop-off addresses, and route data — removed from Firestore.

Device & Tokens

Push notification tokens, device info, and session data — fully wiped.

What May Be Retained (Legally Required)

  • Legal dispute records — if your account is involved in an ongoing investigation or dispute, data may be retained until resolution.
  • Anonymized analytics — aggregated, non-personal data that cannot be linked back to you.

Deletion Timeline

Day 0 – Request Received

We acknowledge your deletion request via email within 24 hours.

Within 7 Days – Data Erased

Your Firebase Auth account, Firestore documents, and all linked data are permanently deleted.

Day 7 – Confirmation Sent

You receive a final confirmation email that your data has been erased.

Under GDPR, we have up to 30 days to comply. We aim to complete all deletions within 7 days.
